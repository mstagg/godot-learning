# 0026 · Level 2
Goal: Clearing the wall takes me to level 2, a box of bricks, with the ball back on the paddle and my score and lives kept.
Behaviour:
- Each level is a scene: scenes/levels/level_1.tscn (the three groups) and level_2.tscn (the box), a Node2D running scripts/level.gd (class_name Level) with Brick instances placed in the editor. Generated once from the 0025 text layouts; the text layouts go.
- brick.gd and pixel_nine_patch.gd are @tool, so the editor draws each brick at its real size with its art.
- level.gd counts its Brick children in _ready, re-emits their points as points_scored, and emits cleared, deferred, when the last one goes.
- game.gd holds @export var levels: Array[PackedScene] and loads levels[(level - 1) % levels.size()] through the play area's load_level(scene), so levels wrap while the label counts on.
- load_level() frees the old level, adds the new one, relays its signals up and puts the ball back on the centred paddle. The real size arriving only places walls, drain and paddle.
- On level_cleared, game.gd does _level += 1 and loads it; score and lives carry over; no new state.
Done when:
- With starting_level 2, the game starts on the box.
- Clearing level 1 shows "Level 2", the box and the ball held on the centred paddle, with score and lives kept.
- Game over on level 2, then Space, gives "Level 1" and the three groups.
- level_2.tscn opens in the editor and shows the box.
Not in this spec:
- Resources (LevelData)
- More levels
- Per-level settings, backgrounds or behaviour
- A level-clear message or pause
