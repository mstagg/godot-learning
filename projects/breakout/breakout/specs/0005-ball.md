# 0005 · Bouncing ball
Goal: A ball moves around the play area and bounces off the walls and the paddle.
Behaviour:
- Ball is a circle 12px across (radius 6), its own scene (ball.tscn / ball.gd), instanced in main.
- Ball root is a CharacterBody2D and moves with move_and_collide(), reflecting off whatever it hits.
- At start it sits at the centre of the screen and moves down-right at 45°.
- Speed is a constant 200 px/s and never changes after a bounce; radius and speed are @export vars.
- The paddle bounces it like a mirror, the same as a wall.
- The paddle ignores the ball (collision layers: walls 1, paddle 2, ball 3); the ball still bounces off both.
- If the ball gets past the paddle it leaves through the bottom and is gone (re-run the game from the editor to restart).
Done when:
- The ball starts at the centre, moving down-right.
- It bounces off the left, right and top walls, and off the paddle when I catch it.
- In the Remote tab, the ball's velocity stays at ±141.4 on x and y (200 px/s) after every bounce.
- If I miss, it leaves through the bottom and doesn't come back.
Not in this spec:
- Aiming the bounce by where it hits the paddle
- Launching with a key
- Drain, lives, or respawn
- Speeding up over time
- Bricks, score, sound
