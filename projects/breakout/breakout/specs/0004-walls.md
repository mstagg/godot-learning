# 0004 · Colliding Walls
Goal: I need 3 walls. These walls exist to  contain the play space and prevent the balls from leaving the play area. The walls should be on the top, left, and right of the screen. The ball should  bounce off of them and the paddle should not travel past it.

Behaviour:
- Walls exist on the top, left, and right of the screen. 
- Walls should extend the length of the 3 designated screen edges.
- Paddle should not move past the walls, IE it collides with side walls
- Walls should be 10px thick

Done when:
- Running the game shows three visible walls

Not in this spec:
- Ball, drain, or paddle changes
