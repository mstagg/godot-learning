# Art background: miniature painter, digital novice

Learner paints miniatures and calls themselves "decent among regular people, bad among artists". Traditional skills (colour, value, contrast, edge highlighting, shading) can be assumed and used as analogies. Digital art is entirely new: editors, layers, pixel-grid technique, palettes, animation frames and sprite sheets all need teaching from scratch. Art lessons are now in scope, but stay basic and serve the clones.
